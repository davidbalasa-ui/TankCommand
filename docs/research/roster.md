# Roster (humans: United Terran Federation "UTF"; aliens: Vrass "VS")

Evidence status: almost nothing beyond the spec's confirmed list was findable (see sources.md). Everything below is either from the sources listed or marked Unknown / Decided. The original's proper unit names are Unknown; no list is published in anything reachable.

## Confirmed facts (with sources)
- Two factions, near-symmetric; all vehicles/aircraft, no infantry; most fire on the move. Source: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armor_Command), as cited in the spec.
- Vrass signature: flying saucers, "very powerful, devastating against enemies on the ground and in the air", obtainable by "upgrading" (tech) quickly. Source: https://www.cyberly.org/game-cheats/pc/armor-command.html
- Heli-pad launches fighters and bombers; enemy vehicles can target aircraft and track them home. Source: same Cyberly page.
- Scouts are cheap, used on point. Source: same.
- Packable miners and gun batteries; radar tower; sensor symbols beyond detail range. Source: GameSpot review (https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/armor-command-review/1900-2538028/), via spec.
- Build menu groups units into offensive and defensive. Source: Old PC Gaming via spec.
