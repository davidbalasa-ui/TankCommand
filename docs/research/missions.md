Evidence status: only two mission titles and the structure are known. WebFetch was blocked (see sources.md), so no walkthrough was read. Everything else is Unknown.

## Structure (sourced)
- Five training missions; each side has its own training set (GameSpot summary, https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/armor-command-review/1900-2538028/; Wikipedia says five).
- Two "Tour of Duty" campaigns, 22 missions each (GameSpot; Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armor_Command). Full-motion video between missions.
- Optional missions exist (YouTube title below).
- Mission maps were criticised as poorly designed (Micro Mart, https://everygamegoing.com/larticle/armour-command/54849, fragment).
- Human campaign: push aliens back to wormhole, destroy them, study the wormhole. Alien campaign: invade Earth, enslave people (spec, from Wikipedia).
