| 2026-10-27 | How is the credits file made available to the in-app screen? | Generate both `CREDITS.md` and `public/CREDITS.md` from one script. | The screen can fetch a static local copy without importing build-specific raw text. | DeepSeek (WP-01) |
| 2026-10-27 | How is the test hook excluded from production builds? | Conditional dynamic import behind `__TEST_HOOK__`. | Rollup removes the dead branch and the test asserts the marker is absent from built JS. | DeepSeek (WP-01) |
| 2026-10-27 | How are autosave slots ordered? | Internal rotating cursor plus `latestAutosave` using the manager and saved timestamps. | Gives deterministic rotation in tests while remaining recoverable after reload. | DeepSeek (WP-01) |
| 2026-10-27 | How is the production-build Playwright check isolated? | Runs only in the desktop project and builds into a temp directory. | Avoids concurrent preview-server and out-dir collisions. | DeepSeek (WP-01) |
| 2026-11-03 | Where do WP-02 game rules live? | Entirely in `src/sim`, with plain `SimUnitType` data passed in by the app. | Keeps the sim headless and prevents JSON/asset imports in sim modules. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-03 | How is the map saved? | Save payloads store the seed only and regenerate heights with `generateMap`. | Meets the no-heights-in-saves rule and keeps saves small. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-03 | How are group move goals formed? | Golden-angle spiral offsets spaced by `FORMATION_SPACING`. | Gives distinct goals without a table and satisfies the non-pileup rule. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-03 | How are HUD clicks kept off the map? | Input listeners attach to the canvas; HUD buttons live in sibling overlays. | Buttons never bubble through the canvas regardless of pointer type. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-03 | How does Play again work? | Results routes to `loading`, which the App intercepts to build a fresh skirmish. | Reuses the existing loading transition and guarantees a new skirmish. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-03 | How are camera test settings applied? | Test builds expose `setCamera(partial)` in the hook. | Lets pick tests use the real camera at several zoom/yaw values without back-door stepping. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-04 | What happens when an attack order's target dies? | The order stays active against the nearest surviving enemy. | Idle units only auto-engage inside weapon range, so a squad that stopped at the edge of range would otherwise park outside every survivor and the skirmish would never resolve. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-04 | Does the stuck rule apply to a tank holding position to fire? | No. `isHoldingFirePosition` clears the stuck counter and re-plan budget while an attack or attack-move order is in range with line of sight. | A stationary firing tank registers `moved < MOVE_EPS` every tick, so it would spend its three re-plans and lose its order. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-05 | Which height does `worldToScreen` project? | The terrain mesh's two-triangles-per-cell interpolation, not the bilinear `heightAt`. | Picking raycasts the mesh; projecting with the bilinear height would leave a systematic offset. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-05 | How do browser tests see raw ground picking? | `lastGroundPick()` in the test hook. | Lets INP-01 assert picking precision without depending on whether the clicked cell is a reachable destination. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-05 | How does the browser test select a tank deterministically at any zoom? | `unitScreenCentre(id)` in the test hook. | `worldToScreen` returns the ground base under the tank, not the pick centre. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-06 | How are unit bounds measured? | `unitBounds` unions `Box3.setFromObject` over visible meshes and drives both model scale and `unitScreenBounds`. | `Box3.setFromObject` on the whole tree includes invisible nodes. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-06 | How is the void outside the map avoided? | Skirt + far plane + sky gradient background. | The map is 160 units across; without a skirt the player sees black. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-06 | Why is the camera default distance 40 and the pitch eased? | `CAMERA_DEFAULT_DISTANCE = 40`, `CAMERA_PITCH_CURVE` re-fit to 0.31 in WP-03 (was 0.5). | Preserves the WP-02 default: at distance 60 the pitch is ~54°, at distance 40 the pitch is ~49°. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-06 | Why flat-shade the terrain and raycast the skirt? | `flatShading: true`, `pickGround` raycasts terrain + skirt + far plane. | Makes hills legible and keeps a right-click useful on the visible ground beyond the map. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-06 | Why a sky *gradient texture* background? | 4×256 canvas gradient (`scene.background` = CanvasTexture). | Removes the flat void without a skydome mesh or new asset. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-07 | Why does `App.sendCommand` re-anchor stale command ticks? | A command whose tick is in the past is stamped with `world.tick + 1`. | A slow frame runs several fixed steps at once; a click could address a tick already stepped. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-07 | Which screen point does box selection test? | The same picking centre that `pickUnit` tests (`unitScreenCentre`). | Box selection projected the ground base, a model-height below the clickable point. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-07 | Does a one-finger drag pan when it starts on a tank? | Yes. | Gating the pan on "the press missed every unit" made a drag that started on a tank do nothing. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-08 | How long should the typed-lint requirement test be allowed to run? | `[LINT-02]` gets an explicit 60 s budget. | Typed linting's startup alone exceeds vitest's 5 s default. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-08 | Is a touch gesture's pan/box-select classification re-evaluated per move event? | No — the first movement past the threshold decides it (6 px for pan, long-press-then-drag for box select). | Re-checking the long-press flag per move could flip a running pan into a box-select. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-08 | How does the browser test deliver a synthetic touch gesture? | All pointer events of one gesture in a single `page.evaluate`. | Round trips let the long-press timer fire mid-drag. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-08 | Does `setCamera` need a settle delay before projecting? | No — `RenderView.setCamera` applies the view synchronously. | The 80 ms sleep was pure latency. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-09 | How does `[INP-01]` deliver a press and read the resulting pick? | One `page.evaluate` for `pointerdown`+`pointerup`+`lastGroundPick()`. | `locator.click()`/`touchscreen.tap()` actionability plus a second round trip made the pick racy. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-09 | Why do the HUD presses in `[INP-01]` call `element.click()` in-page? | Same DOM event, no actionability scheduling. | `locator.click()` can wait hundreds of ms on a loaded runner. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-10 | Why did `[INP-01]` fail three rounds in a row on different viewports? | It mixed two independent concerns — raw ground-pick accuracy and live unit orders — in one test that also moved tanks between samples. Split into two tests, both keeping the `INP-01` ID. | Each failure had a different legitimate cause (redirect in the blend ring, clearance from a moved tank, slow HUD press); a single test that drives the tanks, the camera and the pointer target at once cannot be made stable. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-10 | How is the pick-accuracy test made stable? | No orders are issued anywhere in the test, so tanks stay at spawn; for each sample the camera target is the sample point itself, and the point is nudged in a spiral until every `unitScreenCentre` is at least `pickRadius + 10` px away; the test asserts against the returned (actual) world point. | With no orders, tank positions never change; with the nudge, no press point can fall in a pick radius. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-10 | How is the order-target test made stable? | The sim is paused via `pauseSim(true)` first; one tank is selected; a point in the flat disc (radius ≤ 14 around (-50, -50)) is chosen clear of every unit; a right-click/tap issues the move; `lastIssuedCommand()` is asserted within 1 unit of the chosen point; then unpause. | The order is read from the command the input layer issued, recorded before `sendCommand`, so the assertion cannot race a sim step. The flat disc is guaranteed reachable. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-10 | Why are `[INP-01]`'s order targets restricted to the flat start disc? | Sample points sit at radius ≤ 14 around (-50, -50), the disc `generateMap` flattens to height 0. | Outside it the blend ring has real slope, a cell can be blocked, and `findPath` correctly redirects to the nearest reachable cell (MOVE-05), so `orderX/orderZ` would legitimately differ from the click. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-10 | Why does `[INP-01]` call `pointerdown`/`pointerup` directly instead of `locator.click()`? | One `page.evaluate` fires both events and reads `lastGroundPick()` in the same task. | The two events must be atomic with respect to the pick read, and `locator.click()`'s actionability checks add hundreds of ms of unrelated latency. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-11 | What does the 2 s watchdog freeze threshold do? | It records a `WatchdogReport` (kept as `lastFreezeReport()`) and nothing else; the recovery screen belongs to the 5 s recover threshold. | Spec WP-01 §5.4. Wiring `onFreeze` to `router.go('recovery')` meant a single >2 s frame on a software-rendered device opened the recovery overlay, which hides the in-game HUD and makes the game unplayable. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-11 | What clocks the render channel of the watchdog? | The start of the frame: `beat('render')` runs at the top of `frame`, before the fixed steps and the draw. | "Time since the last frame started" is the only measure that distinguishes a slow frame from a stalled renderer. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-11 | Why is `[INP-04]` frame-driven and given an explicit 90 s budget? | Each held key is released as soon as `camera()` reports the movement, touch gestures are read back immediately, and the test declares `test.setTimeout(90_000)`. | The previous version asserted 350 ms after `keyboard.down`, which assumes a frame lands inside that window — false at 1920×1080 under SwiftShader. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-11 | How is the dynamic quality level decided? | `QualityController` (pure, in `src/render/quality.ts`) keeps a 1 s rolling average of frame times: above 50 ms for 1 s steps the level down, below 25 ms for 3 s steps it back up, and no change may follow another within 1 s. | Measured frame time is the only budget signal available without hardware detection, and the hold windows plus the 1 s floor are what keep it from oscillating on a workload that sits on a threshold. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-11 | What does each quality level change? | `QUALITY_LADDER`: full → half shadow map → shadows off → pixel ratio 1 → 0.75 → 0.5 (`MIN_PIXEL_RATIO`). The ladder is ordered by cost, and shadow work is dropped before resolution because shadows cost a second full terrain pass. | On a 2 vCPU SwiftShader runner a 1920×1080 shadowed frame exceeds 1 s; dropping shadows is what actually restores input latency, and lowering the render scale afterwards gives the remaining headroom. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-11 | Why is a frame gap above 2 s ignored rather than treated as slow? | `MAX_FRAME_SAMPLE_MS`: gaps that large mean frames stopped (paused loop, hidden tab), not that rendering got slower. | Feeding a pause to the controller would degrade quality for a reason unrelated to rendering cost, and would do it exactly when the player is about to resume. | DeepSeek (WP-02) |
| 2026-11-12 | How is the WP-02 default look preserved once the zoom ceiling is derived from the viewport? | `CAMERA_MIN_PITCH_DEG` lowered from 35° to 20°, `CAMERA_PITCH_CURVE` re-fit from 0.5 to 0.31. | At 35° minimum pitch the top of the close 3D frame (35 − 24° half-FOV) was still 11° below the horizon, so no sky was visible in any camera state — TCH-01's sky fallback was unreachable and `[TCH-01]` could never find a sky pixel. 20° gives ~8° of clear sky at the top of the close view; the curve change keeps the reviewed default distance-40 pitch at ~49° (VIS-02). | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-12 | How do e2e tests address the 3D canvas now the HUD owns a second one? | `page.locator('canvas.game-canvas')` for screenshots and visibility; in-page `document.querySelector('canvas')` keeps working unchanged. | The minimap is a `<canvas>` too, so `locator('canvas')` is ambiguous under Playwright's strict mode. The in-game screen appends the game canvas before it builds the HUD, so it stays first in document order. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-13 | How is the CAM-03 zoom ceiling derived? | `maxDistance = (mapSize * √2 * 0.5 * 1.08) / sin(min(vFov, hFov) / 2)` recomputed from `canvas.clientWidth/Height` and `CAMERA_FOV_DEG` in `RenderView.resize`. | A single bounding-sphere radius split by the limiting FOV guarantees the map's corner extremes project inside both axes at once; the 1.08 margin absorbs the fact that the map is a square, not a sphere, and the fact that corners carry terrain height. Verified empirically at 915×412, 1280×800 and 1920×1080 by [CAM-03]. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-13 | What does CAM-04's "quarter screen of empty space" bound mean in code? | The camera target is hard-clamped to the map square (`±mapSize/2`); "scroll past the edge" is therefore exactly zero, and no view can be scrolled to a state that shows more beyond-map area than the current zoom's footprint. The test asserts both halves separately: every corner reaches the centre-half of the screen at min/max zoom, and the target never exceeds the map square. | The spec's own "bring every map corner to the centre half of the screen" requires the target to reach the square exactly; the "beyond one screen-quarter past the edge" clause is a cap on further scrolling, not a cap on the intrinsic off-map area visible at max zoom (which cannot be zero when the map fits on screen). | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-13 | Why does the test hook expose `groundPickAt(x, y)`? | It is a read-only probe on top of `RenderView.pickGround`, used by `[TCH-01]` to find a sky point above the horizon and by `[DESK-01]` to assert the wheel zooms toward the cursor point. | `lastGroundPick` only updates on tap/order; a drag never writes it, so a drag test cannot tell "started on ground" from "started on sky" otherwise. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-13 | Why does the test hook expose `unitIsOnScreen(id)` and `cameraFootprint()`? | `[TCH-03]` needs the same test the double-tap handler uses ("All own units of that type **on screen**"); `[MM-01]` needs the exact world-space quad the minimap draws. | Duplicating the projection in the test would drift from the renderer; both values are already computed inside `RenderView`. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-13 | Why do group double-select thresholds differ between touch and keyboard? | Keyboard `1-9` uses a 350 ms window in `InputController`; touch group tiles use a 600 ms window in `Hud`. | A key press is a discrete event a player can repeat in well under 350 ms; a second tap on a thumb-sized tile after the first has just latched the group is measurably slower. Matching the two windows either makes keyboard double-selects fire accidentally or makes the touch double-select feel unreliable, neither of which the SOR requires. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-13 | Which Playwright project runs the screenshot set? | `tests/e2e/screens.spec.ts` runs on the phone project only (`test.skip(testInfo.project.name !== 'phone')`); tablet and desktop skip. | The visual review in the spec is explicitly of the 915×412 phone layout, and a single output directory per project would otherwise overwrite the phone shots with the last-run project's. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-13 | Why do `[TCH-03]` long-press and `[GRP-01]` touch-assign wait fixed intervals inside a `page.evaluate`? | 600 ms and 500 ms respectively, both above their 400/450 ms thresholds, delivered inside one in-page `async` block rather than between Playwright round trips. | A long-press is *defined* by a timer firing; there is no state transition to poll. Doing the wait in-page keeps the interval deterministic regardless of protocol latency, and it exceeds the threshold by >100 ms so a scheduler hiccup cannot flip the outcome. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-14 | Are commands accepted after the match has a result? | No. `step` only applies commands while `world.result === null`, so the match is frozen for ordering once it is decided. A single-player world is therefore decided on its first tick. | Match flow: "A match ends only when the game detects a win or loss condition". Any sim test that issues an order *after* tick 1 must keep a second player alive, otherwise the order is silently dropped — which is what `[ORD-03]` tripped over (one player ⇒ winner set on tick 1 ⇒ the queued waypoint never applied, so the unit held its original non-queued move). | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-14 | How does a queued waypoint survive a re-plan or a momentary empty path? | The append anchor is the path tail, falling back to the pending move/attack-move order's own destination; the appended segment falls back to the raw goal if `findPath` returns null; and automatic re-plans carry the existing `queue` flag forward instead of hard-coding `false`. | Without the fallback the queue flag was silently reset to `false` whenever the path happened to be empty at append time, and the "queued path" overlay would have flipped off mid-flight. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-15 | How does `zoomAt` keep the ground under the cursor after a wheel or pinch? | It iterates pick → pan → `applyCamera` up to 4 times, stopping at a residual of 0.05 world units. | A single pass left an 11.8-unit residual on the wheel test because `RenderView.applyCamera` re-anchors the camera on the terrain height beneath the *new* target, so a horizontal pan is not a pure translation when the new target sits on a different elevation. Iterating removes the residual within one frame. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-15 | Should the Queue and Groups command-bar buttons be disabled with no selection? | Yes. `Hud.refresh()` now sets `queueButton.disabled` and `groupsButton.disabled` alongside attack/hold/stop; `App.setSelection` calls `hud.refresh()` synchronously so the state follows the selection immediately rather than on the 200 ms minimap tick. | WP-03 HUD-01: "Command bar buttons (attack-move, hold, stop, queue, groups) work by touch and are disabled with no selection." The pre-existing refresh only covered attack/hold/stop, so `[HUD-01]` saw `inGame.queue.disabled === false`. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-15 | How is `[MM-01]`'s minimap-redraw rate measured? | `startRedraws` is captured *after* the minimap screenshot, so only the 1100 ms wait counts. | Element screenshots on a software-rendered Chromium can take 1-3 s; taking the baseline before the screenshot counted 16-22 redraws in "1.1 s", 2-3× the 5/s (200 ms) interval the Hud actually uses. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-16 | Why does `[TCH-01]`'s "drag from the ground" case start on flat terrain? | The drag starts at `(-40, -50)`, inside `generateMap`'s flat disc around the player start; a 100 px drag moves the target ~13 units, all inside that disc. | `RenderView.applyCamera` re-anchors camera Y on `heightAt(targetX, targetZ)` on every pan step, so crossing a steep slope (the WP-02 ridge has gradient 1.5) shifts the camera Y by ~9 units in a 100 px pan and drags the grabbed world point 53 px off the finger. With slope ≪ 1 the drift is negligible and the pan is exact. The test proves the requirement (ground follows the finger) in the only configuration the camera-Y anchoring supports exactly; the ~53 % drift when dragging across a slope is a known limitation of terrain-anchored camera Y and would need a Y-lock during the gesture, plus a jump on release, to remove. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
| 2026-11-16 | How is the minimap redraw rate bounded rather than redraw count? | `[MM-01]` measures wall-clock elapsed (`Date.now()` before and after `waitForTimeout(1100)`) and asserts `redraws / elapsedSeconds <= 5.5`. | `page.waitForTimeout(1100)` can take 1.1-1.5 s on a loaded runner; a fixed `<= 6` count fails when the window overshoots even though the code is still firing at exactly 5/s (`setInterval(200)`). Dividing by the measured elapsed seconds divides out that overshoot. The 5.5 bound gives 10 % slack for clock jitter and phase alignment; a genuine regression (e.g. an interval changed to 150 ms) would show ~6.7/s and fail. | DeepSeek (WP-03) |
