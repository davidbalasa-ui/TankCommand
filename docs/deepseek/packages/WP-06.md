# Work package WP-06: diplomacy, fog of war, sensors, radar and stealth

Adds the SOR "Vision" section. This must land before the enemy AI (WP-07), because the AI may only use
what its own units can see.

## 0. Diplomacy (src/sim/diplomacy.ts) — do this first; everything below and WP-07/WP-09 use it

The owner wants room to add later: more than two factions, neutral non-player units (miners, monsters,
citizens), allies, and missions such as "protect the citizens". None of those are built now, but the
engine must not assume "two sides, one enemy". Replace every "owner !== mine means enemy" check in the
sim, AI, fog, UI and renderer with relation queries.

- A **side** is any player id with a faction, a controller (`human`, `ai`, `scripted`, `none`), and a
  team. Support up to 8 sides. A neutral side (`controller: 'scripted'` or `'none'`) is a normal side
  that nobody commands unless a mission trigger does.
- A **relation** exists for every ordered pair of sides: `allied`, `neutral` or `hostile`. Stored in
  world state (saved), symmetric by default but settable per direction. Same team = allied.
- API: `relation(world, a, b)`, `isHostile(world, a, b)`, `isAllied(world, a, b)`. Units auto-engage only
  hostile targets. Allied sides share vision (merge their vision grids for visibility and the minimap) and
  can't damage each other, including splash. Neutral sides neither attack nor are auto-attacked.
- **Provocation**: a command `diplomacy.set` (side a, side b, relation) exists for triggers and the AI.
  Per-pair `provokedBy` rule (data, default on): when a neutral side's unit or structure is damaged by
  side X, relation neutral→hostile toward X (that side only, not X's allies) and its units may defend
  themselves; an optional `forgiveAfterSec`. When armed neutral units defend, only they and their
  structures react; others stay neutral unless data says the neutral side is one group (`groupId`),
  in which case the whole group turns hostile.
- Victory and elimination (WP-04) count only sides with `controller` `human` or `ai`; neutral sides never
  block or cause victory by themselves. Team victory: a team wins when every other team is eliminated.
- Perception, the fog code and the AI all go through these queries (the AI never reads another side's
  hidden data, as before).
- Skirmish setup keeps its two-sides default; add a data-only way to start a world with extra neutral
  sides and non-default relations (used by tests and later by missions).
- UI: faction colours come from the side; a neutral side's units are drawn in a neutral colour; hostile
  units red on the minimap, allied blue-green, neutral grey.

| --- | --- |

## 1. Vision in the sim (src/sim/vision.ts)

- Per side, a grid of cells (map cell size) with three states: **unexplored**, **explored** (seen
  before, not now), **visible** (seen now). Recomputed at most every 4 ticks (placeholder), from every
  unit and structure's sight range.
- **Line of sight** blocks vision: a cell is visible only if terrain doesn't block the line from the
  viewer's eye height to the cell. Use the same terrain ray test as combat. High ground sees farther
  (eye height from terrain).
- **Sensor range** (beyond sight): enemy units inside a side's sensor range but outside its sight show as
  **anonymous contacts** (position only, no type, owner shown as "unknown"). Radar towers and a mobile
  **Radar** unit (placeholder row) extend sensor range.
- **Stealth** (the WP-05 upgrade): a stealthed unit is never a sensor contact and is only visible within
  a reduced sight range (data multiplier).
- **Explored memory**: structures last seen in explored cells are remembered as "last known" ghosts
  until seen again.
- A per-side **perception query** API in the sim: `perceivedUnits(world, side)` returns only what that
  side may know (visible units with full data, contacts with position only, remembered structures).
  Everything outside the sim that wants enemy information (renderer, HUD, minimap, AI) must use it.

## 2. Rendering and UI (phone first)

- Fog drawn on the terrain: unexplored black, explored dimmed, visible normal. A cheap approach: a
  small data texture of the vision grid sampled by the terrain material, updated when the grid changes.
- Hidden enemy units are **not drawn**, not pickable, not in the minimap, and not in any test-hook
  output meant for the player view. Contacts are drawn as a pulsing neutral symbol, on the map and the
  minimap.
- Settings toggle "Fog of war: on / revealed" (skirmish option later), default on.
