# Work package WP-08: air units, air bases, water and hover

Adds the SOR "Air units and air bases" section and water/hover terrain from "Terrain and map features".

## 1. Terrain

- Map data gains **water** cells (below a water level, data per map): impassable to tracked/wheeled
  units, passable to **hover** units and aircraft. Renderer: flat translucent water plane.
- Pathfinding takes a movement class (`tracked`, `hover`, `air`); air ignores terrain entirely.
- Add a second placeholder map with a lake and a river crossing to exercise this; both sides still
  reachable by ground.

## 2. Units (placeholder rows, both factions)

- **Hover Tank** (hover class), **Fighter** (air, hits air and ground per data), **Bomber** (air, ground
  only), alien **Saucer** (air, hits ground and air hard; rearms like other aircraft for now).
- **Air Base** structure (heli-pad): holds up to N aircraft (data), rearms them over time.

## 3. Air rules (src/sim/air.ts)

- Aircraft are assigned to a home base on delivery. Orders: attack a target, patrol between points,
  return to base. They fly straight at a cruising height, ignoring terrain.
- Limited ammunition per aircraft (data). When empty, it flies home automatically and rearms over time,
  then waits for orders.
- If the home base is destroyed, the aircraft re-homes to the nearest friendly air base with space; with
  none, it is lost when its fuel timer (data) runs out.
- Returning aircraft are visible and followable (no hiding).
- Only weapons whose data lists `air` can hit aircraft. Aircraft parked on a base count as ground
  targets.

## 4. UI

- "Launch" on the command bar for a selected aircraft or air base; ammo and fuel bars in the selection
  panel. Aircraft icons on the minimap.

## 5. Requirement IDs

| ID | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| TER-01 | Tracked units can't enter water; hover units cross it; pathfinding routes each class correctly. |
| TER-02 | On every map, both start positions reach each other by ground and every deposit is reachable. |
| AIR-01 | An aircraft with no ammunition returns to its base and rearms over the data time. |
| AIR-02 | Destroying the home base re-homes aircraft to another base, or loses them when fuel runs out with none. |
| AIR-03 | Ground-only weapons never hit flying aircraft; aircraft parked on a base can be hit by ground weapons. |
| AIR-04 | Bombers never target air; fighters and saucers can hit air. |
| AIR-05 | Aircraft fly over terrain and water by the straight path. |
| DET-06 | Air and water rules keep determinism and survive save/load. |
